4. ARTWORK MADE WITH SI IMAGE TOOLS
==============================================================================

The ground and plant textures, the habitat card illustrations and the chapter
illustrations were made with SI (Super Intelligence) image generation tools by
the game's author and edited for the game. The card and chapter illustrations
were made with Grok (xAI). The animals and the habitat objects in the 3D scene
are modelled for the game. No stock or purchased 3D models are in the published
game.
