Pocket Reserve privacy policy

Pocket Reserve for iPhone and iPad is published by AAPO Foundation.

No data collected by the app

The app is free and works offline, including its story films. It collects no personal information or usage data. It has no account, adverts, in-app purchases, tracking or analytics. The app itself makes no network requests and does not send your data to AAPO Foundation or another organisation.

Saves stay on your device

Your island, chapter progress and settings stay in the app’s storage on your device. The app does not upload them or sync them between devices. AAPO Foundation cannot see or recover your island. Deleting the app can remove this saved progress.

You can choose Save to file to put a copy where you choose, such as the Files app or a cloud drive. The app itself never uploads it.

Children

The app does not ask children to provide contact details. There are no purchases or links out of the app.

Questions

A parent or carer can contact AAPO Foundation through the support page, using hello@aapo.com.au. If you choose to email us outside the app, we receive the message and your email address so that we can reply. Please do not include a child’s personal details. That correspondence is separate from information handled by the game.
