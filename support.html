Pocket Reserve support

Pocket Reserve is published by AAPO Foundation.

Help with the game

Choose a habitat card, then choose a place on the island. An animal moves in when its needs are met. The Nature notebook helps explain what each animal needs. Open Chapters to read a chapter again or find Story so far.

Your island

The iOS app is free and works offline, with no adverts or in-app purchases. No account is needed. The app collects no data, makes no network requests and blocks all links out of the app. Your island and progress are saved on the device. AAPO Foundation does not hold a copy of your save and cannot restore a lost island. Deleting the app can remove its saved progress; please do not reinstall it as a first troubleshooting step.

If the picture or sound stops, close and reopen the app. Check the game’s Sound control if a film is silent.

Contact

A parent or carer can contact AAPO Foundation at hello@aapo.com.au. Tell us the app version, the iPhone or iPad model and what happened. Please do not send a child’s name, photo, location or saved game. This support page is outside the app; the app does not open an email or send a report.

Read the privacy policy for how the iOS app handles information.
