How to play

  1. PlantYou start on almost bare rock with eight seeds and a hand of cards: meadow, flowers, hedge, pond and reeds. Each seed plants one card on one tile.
  2. InviteYour Wildlife list shows which animals have a home ready. Pick one, then tap the tile it will live on. First up: an earthworm and a bumblebee!
  3. ConnectChapter by chapter, you join your island up: meadow next to meadow so the wind can’t blow it away, reeds next to water, and trout in the pond so a kingfisher comes to fish.

Every seed can help an animal find a home.

It’s 2052. Far below the clouds, the Earth is resting and slowly getting better. Up above, the floating islands of the Lumen shelf look after plants and animals until they can go home. You look after Cell 7, the furthest island out: bare rock, just waiting for its first green shoots. Twenty-six animals are hoping to move in, from earthworms to otters, all from the meadows, wetlands and woods of Europe’s rainy north-west.

Each chapter gives you a handful of seeds: enough if you plan, but not enough to waste. An animal needs its whole home at once, the ground it lives on plus the cover, water or flowers next to it. A seed in the wrong spot can leave an animal without a home, so think before you plant!

The story has 50 chapters in five acts, from The First Green to Home Ground. Each one starts with a message from a friend on the Lumen shelf.

The story

Meet the twenty-six animals

Each one needs a home just like in the wild.

An earthworm only asks for soft ground. A frog wants water with meadow next to it. A kingfisher is fussier: clear water, a branch to watch from, a bank to dig into and fish close by, or it’s off!

For grown-ups

  • What children practise. Planning ahead with a few seeds, and learning what each animal needs to live: the right ground, with water, cover or flowers next to it.
  • Short sessions. The story comes in small chapters and the island saves by itself, so it is easy to stop and come back later.
  • Sound. The game plays wind, water, a little music and animal calls from the first tap. The Sound button in the game turns it all off.
  • Privacy. No adverts, no purchases, no account and no tracking. The game collects nothing. The island is saved only in this browser and is never sent anywhere. Save to file makes a copy for you to keep.
